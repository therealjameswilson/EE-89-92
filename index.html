Source Overlap

Where This Volume Should Start

Bush Library Memcons and Telcons

Reuse the Western Europe harvest pattern for presidential meetings, telephone calls, summit conversations, and partially released packets with Eastern Europe participants or subject cues.

Brent Scowcroft Papers

Screen Presidential Correspondence, telcon files, subject files, and country folders for conversations that were not captured in the main memcon/telcon FOIA route.

NSC Country and Regional Files

Prioritize Poland, Hungary, Czechoslovakia, Romania, Bulgaria, Albania, Yugoslavia, CSCE, NATO, and Warsaw Pact transition folders.

State Department Records

Use Central Foreign Policy Files, embassy reporting, policy memoranda, and assistance/debt records to fill non-presidential narrative gaps.

Western Europe Crossovers

Mark records where Thatcher, Mitterrand, Kohl, the Vatican, NATO, or EC actors discuss Eastern Europe even when the speaker is outside this volume's country chapters.

Declassification Trail

Keep denied, partial, pending, and newly released records visible so the compiler can separate publication-ready documents from follow-up review targets.

Watch List

Presidential Trip Files

July 1989 Poland and Hungary trip records should be matched against memcons, briefing books, public remarks, and NSC follow-up memoranda.

Non-Presidential Diplomacy

Secretary-level, Eagleburger, Zoellick, Hutchings, embassy, and assistance-policy records are likely essential where leader memcons are sparse.

Yugoslavia

Separate U.S. recognition policy, EC mediation, sanctions, refugee, arms embargo, and Bosnia-related records before assigning final chapter order.

Cross-Volume Records

Capture Eastern Europe references inside Western Europe, Soviet Union, Germany, NATO, and regional security records instead of treating them as out of scope.

Build Plan

Ingest Checklist

  1. 01 Harvest Eastern Europe rows from the Bush Library memcon/telcon route and compare them against the Western Europe data model.
  2. 02 Assign every item to Poland, Hungary, Czechoslovakia, Romania/Bulgaria/Albania, or Yugoslavia/Regional.
  3. 03 Sort chronologically inside each chapter while preserving cross-volume references and source-family tags.
  4. 04 Normalize document title, place/date/time line, participants, countries, release status, NAID, PDF URL, and Source note.
  5. 05 Mark gaps for State Department cables, non-presidential memoranda, assistance files, and denied or partial releases.
  6. 06 Replace compiler cues with fully cited records as each archival packet is confirmed.
